# Design safety and correctness re-audit — 2026-10-18

Scope: current working-tree Pi 5 Corona HAT Direct schematic, PCB, corrected verification tools and both manufacturing ZIPs. **Disposition: fabrication release remains on hold.** No schematic, PCB or manufacturing file was changed by this rerun.

## Current findings

1. **High — insulation qualification remains unresolved.** The corrected geometry checker reproduces **57 same-layer screening failures, 143 cautions and 16 edge-screening failures** across 6,866 qualifying layer/net pairs. It uses native copper boundaries, refilled zones and actual outlines including NPTH holes. HV_OUT is approximately **6.953 mm** from an outline/hole boundary versus the checker's 10 mm edge target; SEC_A is approximately **6.699 mm** from a boundary. Review mounting-hole hardware as well as the outer perimeter. These counts are conservative screening flags, not 73 independently established insulation-standard violations: layers can repeat a physical pair; the checker applies 10 mm to every HV net, and independently combines voltage-envelope endpoints. For example, adjacent pump nets receive a 5 kV screening differential although their common-phase behavior can have a smaller differential. The 0.00305/0.005 mm/V coefficients are legacy screening criteria. Resolve relevant physical gaps and qualify correlated operating/fault voltages, package insulation, creepage, coating/materials, environment and enclosure geometry before release. A native DRC pass does not establish those properties.

2. **High — retained charge under an interrupted bleeder path is reproduced.** The corrected native-derived simulation completes **104 cases** with a 100 pF external load capacitance. With all bleeders intact, every monitored storage node and capacitor differential falls below the 50 V analysis threshold within approximately **0.933 s**. The larger residuals require a deliberately interrupted bleeder bank: an open resistor, cracked solder joint, broken trace/pad or missing part. They are **single-fault results, not nominal discharge behavior**. Opening the C100 bank (R140/R141) with the effectively open secondary leaves up to approximately **1.225 kV after 5 s**; opening C101's bank (R142/R143) leaves up to approximately **70 V**, and opening C111's bank (R162/R163) leaves approximately **68–70 V**. The output can fall below threshold while an internal capacitor remains charged. The effectively open secondary is a separate shutdown assumption and does not necessarily imply a component failure. Physical transformer/bridge shutdown behavior, component tolerances, generic diode model and actual maximum electrode/cable capacitance remain unqualified; the nominal time is not a certified enclosure access delay.

3. **Medium — return-topology regression and instructions are stale; normal sensing topology verified.** A focused fresh-netlist/PCB review and eight-case ngspice comparison confirm that grounding the transformer/multiplier while returning the external load through J61/R90 correctly measures external load current. The current circuit gives 0.1/0.5/1.0 V for 10/50/100 µA. Restoring the former common /HV_RETURN generator/load connection would bypass R90 for the main load loop; at 50 µA the reconstructed former circuit measures approximately −8.67 µA instead. Keep the current circuit and reconcile the Ruby assertion and AGENTS.md with this output-only sensing purpose. Arc/protection qualification and avoiding external ground bypasses remain open. See [return-topology review](return-topology-review.md) and its simulation evidence. No circuit changes were made.

4. **Medium — bleeder requirements and physical circuit disagree.** The native netlist contains 24 multiplier bleeders: two 100 MΩ resistors per 200 MΩ bank. AGENTS.md and the multiplier-sheet note still require three per 300 MΩ bank. The corrected simulation follows the actual two-resistor topology and includes both 82 kΩ low-side sense resistors. Decide which bleeder architecture is authoritative and reconcile instructions/notes, qualification and circuit; this rerun does not change that design choice.

5. **Medium — historical release/procurement evidence is stale.** AGENTS.md still describes M-layout-8/148 mm/191 unconnected items; current native DRC reports no unconnected items. exports/jlcpcb-validation.json still identifies revision K and KiCad 10.0.6 and claims the audit assertions passed, despite the fresh failure above. The dated catalog evidence and physical component qualifications were not refreshed by regenerating archives. In particular, switch contact mapping/load ratings, transformer terminal/insulation qualification, diode repetitive pulse stress, Pi/enclosure fit and HV wiring remain outstanding. Keep production_release false.
